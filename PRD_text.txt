PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENTERGONOMIC DIGITAL TWINLive Mobile Camera-Based Worker Ergonomics & Fatigue-Risk MonitoringPSE Project | Version 1.0 | MVP / Prototype Specification1. Executive SummaryBuild a phone-first application that uses the phone camera to observe a worker in real time, estimate body pose, calculate ergonomic indicators, estimate fatigue risk from temporal posture/movement patterns, and render a live digital-twin representation over the camera view.Target experience: Open app → start camera → point at worker → live pose tracking → digital twin follows the worker → posture and fatigue-risk indicators update → warnings and recommended actions appear.The MVP estimates ergonomic/fatigue risk; it is not a medical diagnostic system and must not claim to clinically diagnose fatigue, injury, or disease.2. Project ContextThe PSE project focuses on worker ergonomics and fatigue in industrial work. Module 3 requires causal variables, links, polarity, feedback loops, leverage points and system archetypes. The digital twin is the proposed leverage point that strengthens the feedback between exposure, risk detection and intervention. fileciteturn2file0L424-L450Worker: reduce ergonomic exposure and receive understandable feedback.Supervisor: see high-risk situations early.Management: obtain measurable ergonomic-risk information.Project goal: demonstrate a live, explainable digital-twin prototype.3. Product GoalsLive camera-based worker pose tracking.Live digital-twin overlay that follows body movement.Real-time ergonomic posture scoring.Rolling fatigue-risk estimation.Explainable warnings showing the body region/factor causing risk.Privacy-first on-device processing by default.Prototype that can run on a phone without cloud inference.4. Core User JourneyOpen application.Grant camera permission.Tap Start Monitoring.Camera preview opens.Detect one worker and body landmarks.Render digital-twin skeleton/3D avatar.Calculate joint/trunk angles continuously.Update ergonomic risk and fatigue-risk scores.Show NORMAL / CAUTION / HIGH RISK states.If high risk persists, show alert and recommended action.Stop/reset and view session summary.5. Privacy & Responsible UseObtain consent before monitoring.Do not identify workers by face in the MVP.Do not store raw video by default.Prefer pose landmarks over identity data.Use 'fatigue risk', not a medical diagnosis.Do not use the prototype as the sole basis for employment/disciplinary decisions.Provide visible Stop/Pause controls.17. Definition of DoneA user can open the app and start the phone camera.A worker is detected live.The digital twin follows the worker.Bending/reaching/squatting changes relevant posture indicators.Sustained risky posture increases ergonomic risk.Fatigue-risk responds to sustained exposure and movement features.High risk produces an understandable warning.The app explains why the risk increased.Core inference works without a cloud connection.Raw camera video is not uploaded in default mode.26.
